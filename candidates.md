# Three candidate identities

Day 2. One paragraph each, each tied to a real condition.

## 1 Mycelium
Time: Present
Place: Underground, beneath a forest floor
Role: I link separate trees into one network and pass nutrients to whichever one needs them most. Nobody sees me, but the whole forest depends on the connections.

## 2 Prism
Time: Past (Newton's experiments, 1660s)
Place: A dark room with one beam of sunlight
Role: I take a single beam that looks plain and show the colors hidden inside it. I make complicated things readable by splitting them into parts.

## 3 Compound Interest
Time: Future
Place: Inside every patient account, running quietly in the background
Role: I reward whoever stays long enough. I start with almost nothing to show, then grow on top of my own growth until the curve bends upward. I do my best work when nobody interrupts me.
